The Retirement page estimates how your retirement savings could grow while you work, and how long they could last once you stop.

## Calculator Inputs

- **Current Age**, **Retirement Age** and **Life Expectancy** set the timeline.
- **Current Pre-Tax Income** and **Annual Raise** estimate your future income.
- **Contribution (% of Income)** is how much of your income you save toward retirement each year.
- **Current Retirement** is the amount you've already saved.
- **Investment Return** and **Inflation Rate** are yearly percentages applied to every year of the projection.
- **Post-Retirement Expenses** is how much you expect to spend each year in retirement, in today's money.

Your inputs are saved in this browser, not on the server.

## Reading the Results

- The two **Estimated Savings** cards show your projected balance at your retirement age and at your life expectancy.
- The **Cumulative Savings** chart shows your balance growing until retirement and then being drawn down.
- The **Retirement by Year** table breaks this down year by year. **Retirement Amount (Today's Money)** adjusts each balance for inflation, so you can compare it to prices now.

These figures are estimates based on steady returns and inflation. Real markets vary from year to year, so treat the results as a guide rather than a promise.
