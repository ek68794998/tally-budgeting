The Net Worth & Assets page tracks what you own and what you owe, and how your net worth changes over time.

## Assets and Liabilities

Each item has a name, a type, a current value and, optionally, an institution. Tally sorts items into these types:

- **Liquid Assets**: cash and investments you can access, such as checking, savings or brokerage accounts.
- **Fixed Assets**: things of lasting value that are hard to sell quickly, such as a home.
- **Personal Items**: belongings with resale value, such as a car.
- **Short-term Liabilities**: debts you'll pay off soon, such as credit card balances.
- **Long-term Liabilities**: debts paid off over years, such as a mortgage or student loan.

Tally doesn't connect to your bank, so update the values yourself whenever you want an accurate picture.

## Accounts and Imports

Liquid assets and liabilities can be used as accounts on the Transactions page. To import a CSV file into an account, set its **Institution** to the bank or provider the file comes from.

## Deactivating Instead of Deleting

If you close an account or sell something, select **Deactivate** instead of deleting it. That keeps it attached to its past transactions.

## Net Worth

Your **net worth** is your assets minus your liabilities. Select **New Snapshot** to record today's net worth. Over time, snapshots build the history shown in the chart. Recording another snapshot on the same day replaces the earlier one.

The distribution charts show how your assets and liabilities break down by type.
