The Budget page compares what you spent against what you planned to spend, using the transactions you've imported or entered.

## Budget Overview

The **Budget Score** rates the last 12 months on a scale from 0 to 10.

- Spending **over** your total budget keeps the score below 5.
- Staying **within** budget starts you at 5, then adds points for leaving room under your budget and for saving part of your income.
- Budgeting more than 90% of your income lowers the score, since it leaves little room for surprises.
- A perfect 10 requires spending at least 15% under budget, saving at least 15% of income, and budgeting no more than 85% of income.

## Quick Pulse

Shows your spending against your budget for **last month** and the **last 12 months**, along with your net income for the same period. Select **View Spending** to see the details.

## Overview

Highlights the budgets that need your attention:

- **Over Budget**: budgets that exceeded their cap this period.
- **New Overages**: budgets that went over their cap since the last period.
- **Above Average Spend**: budgets that spent more than their monthly average but are still within their total.
- **Back on Track**: budgets that returned to target since the last period.

## Categories

Open the **⋯** menu and select **Categories** to organize your budget. Each **category** groups one or more **subcategories**, and transactions are assigned to subcategories. For each subcategory, you can set:

- **Budgeted Amount** and **Budget Frequency**: for example, $1,200 every 12 months for an annual expense.
- **Budget Type**:
  - **Expense** counts as spending.
  - **Income** counts as money earned.
  - **Reallocation** is for money moving between your own accounts, such as credit card payments or transfers. It isn't counted as income or spending.
- **Needs** and **Savings** percentages: how much of this spending goes to each area. Whatever is left counts as **Wants**.

Deleting a category or subcategory marks its transactions as uncategorized. This can't be undone.

## Spending

The Spending page breaks down your spending for any date range, **by category** and **by area** (needs, wants and savings).
